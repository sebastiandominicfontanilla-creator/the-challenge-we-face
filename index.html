The Article

Desire, Mental Health, and the Cost of Living

When Desire Becomes a Problem

In The Necklace by Guy de Maupassant, we meet Mathilde, a woman who is unhappy with her simple life. She dreams of wealth, beautiful things, and a higher social status. Her strong desire for a better life leads her to borrow a beautiful necklace for an important event.

At first, the necklace seems to represent beauty, wealth, and happiness. However, it eventually becomes connected to a serious problem that changes Mathilde's life. Her experience shows how our desires and decisions can have unexpected consequences.

The story teaches us to appreciate what we already have instead of constantly comparing ourselves with others. It also shows that appearances and material things do not always bring true happiness.

The Importance of Mental Health

Just like Mathilde struggles with dissatisfaction, many students face their own personal struggles. Help-Seeking for Mental Health Among Filipino College Students: A Systematic Literature Review focuses on the difficulties Filipino college students experience when dealing with mental health concerns.

Students may experience stress, academic pressure, anxiety, and personal problems. Even when they need professional help, some may hesitate to seek it because of shame, fear of judgment, or the feeling that others may not understand them.

The study also shows that support from friends and family, accessible mental health services, and better understanding of mental health can encourage students to seek help. This reminds us that asking for support is important, especially when problems become difficult to handle alone.

The Struggle of Rising Costs

Another challenge affecting many people is the increasing cost of living. Food, transportation, water, electricity, and other daily expenses continue to put pressure on families and workers. When prices increase while wages do not keep up, people may have to reduce their spending and carefully manage their money.

Students can also feel the effects of this problem. Those who depend on allowances may struggle to pay for transportation, food, school materials, and other needs.

The cost of living crisis is connected to larger issues such as inflation, poverty, unemployment, and limited opportunities. It shows how financial situations can influence the choices people make every day.

How These Challenges Are Connected

Although The Necklace, the mental health study, and the article about the cost of living discuss different topics, they all show how challenges can affect people's decisions and lives.

Mathilde's desire for wealth affects her choices. Students' experiences with stress and fear of judgment can affect whether they seek mental health support. Rising prices can affect how families and students spend their money.

These situations remind us that people make decisions based on the challenges they experience. Understanding these challenges can help us become more aware of ourselves and more understanding toward others.

A Lesson We Can Take With Us

Life does not always go as planned. Sometimes, our desires can lead us to difficult situations. At other times, stress, mental health struggles, or financial problems can make everyday life harder.

What matters is learning how to recognize these challenges and respond to them wisely. We can appreciate what we have, ask for support when we need it, and become more responsible with our decisions and resources.

In the end, these three texts show us that the problems people face may be different, but they can all shape the way we live, think, and make choices.
